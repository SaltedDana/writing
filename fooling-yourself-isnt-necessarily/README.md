I’ve been experimenting with a small mindset trick I came up with while swimming.

Over the last period, whenever I went to the pool, I kept swimming for a few extra laps after I’d already hit my goal. The intention wasn’t performance; it was psychology.

I wanted my brain to log swimming as something enjoyable, like dessert you keep picking at even when you’re full, not like taking a spoonful of cough syrup you rush through because it’s “good for you.”

I believe tiny changes in how we log an activity can completely change our perception and motivation around it, and I’m testing that now. I will keep you posted.
